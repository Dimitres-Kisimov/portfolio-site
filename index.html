  • A from-scratch, provider-agnostic tool-use loop (mock or Claude) parses an RFQ email, resolves SKUs, checks stock and drafts a quote - number of tool calls varies per email, which is what makes it genuinely agentic.

Replayed on the reliability benchmark's exact fault schedule, it turns every injected content fault detectable: at the anchor tier with 2 retries, delivered-correct 88.7% -> 98.8% and silent-wrong quotes 11,523/yr -> 0, at a visible price - human escalations 260 -> 1,300/yr, tokens ~$843 -> ~$939/yr - with 0 false alarms on clean runs.
